# Existing enemy art

Five BVN enemy forms: zako_ninja1, zako_ninja_thunder1, zako_ninja_earth1, zako_ninja2 and zako_dog1. Existing source-derived HD atlases are preserved under the private project `assets/sprites_hd/enemies/` and packed without redrawing into `atlas.png` by `tools/build-enemy-pack.py`. Atlas offsets, original source paths, animation counts and limitations are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Source: 5DPLAY Game Studio, BleachVsNaruto FlashSrc / OpenBleachVsNaruto:
- https://github.com/5DPLAY-Game-Studio/BleachVsNaruto_FlashSrc
- https://gitlab.com/5dplay-game-studio/OpenBleachVsNaruto

Original character and game artwork rights remain with their authors and rightsholders. This is existing fan-project study material; source/project availability does not imply an independent commercial artwork license. Full collection credits are in the root `assets/CREDITS.md`.

The wolf has five standing/breathing frames and two falling frames. There are no independent running, biting or hit-reaction frames in this prepared atlas; movement and hit reactions therefore use the existing standing cycle rather than invented frames.
